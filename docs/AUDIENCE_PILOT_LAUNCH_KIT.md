# Phase 9E real-audience pilot launch kit

Phase 9E turns one unsigned Phase 9D decision brief into a practical research and
marketing preparation kit. It answers four questions before TradePulse reaches a
real audience: who to invite, what to ask, how to describe the product, and what
evidence determines whether to continue.

## First audience

Use five balanced cohorts rather than one convenient friend group:

| Cohort | Target | Learning objective |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Self-directed retail investors | 6 | Does the guided flow improve independent research? |
| Active market-research users | 6 | How does the evidence workflow compare with current tools? |
| Finance and data professionals | 6 | Are source, freshness and uncertainty labels credible? |
| Globally exposed SME operators | 6 | Are currency, commodity and country-event signals useful for business context? |
| Finance learners and educators | 6 | Is the product clear without creating unsafe investment expectations? |

The ceiling remains 30 participants over four weeks. Participation is voluntary,
free and limited to clearly labelled reference, delayed or curated demo data.

## Questions to ask

Run a 20–25 minute facilitated session. Ask in this order and avoid selling while
the participant is answering:

1. How do you currently understand a market move or world event?
2. What is the hardest or most time-consuming part of that process?
3. Looking at the first screen, what do you believe TradePulse helps you do?
4. Use the guided demo to explain one market move in your own words.
5. Which source, freshness or uncertainty labels do you notice, and what do they mean?
6. What would make you trust this more, and what claim would make you distrust it?
7. What was most useful, and what felt confusing or unnecessary?
8. When, if ever, would you use this in a normal week?
9. What tool or manual process would TradePulse replace or sit beside?
10. If it reliably solved that job, what outcome would make it valuable enough to pay for?
11. What is the single most important change before you would recommend another session?

Do not request or enter participant names, email addresses, phone numbers, account
identifiers or raw interview notes in TradePulse. Use the existing privacy-safe
debrief and aggregate synthesis workflow.

## Market position

**Headline:** Understand global market signals without the data overload.

**Promise:** TradePulse connects market movement, country context and world-event
evidence in one guided research experience.

**Proof:** Show the four-stop demo, transparent source and freshness labels, and
the evidence behind each insight.

**Boundary:** Research and education only. Never market guaranteed predictions,
personalised advice, a licensed real-time feed, autonomous trading or returns that
the product cannot substantiate.

## Founder-led marketing order

1. Begin with ten relevant people from trusted professional and personal networks.
2. Publish learning-led LinkedIn posts with one labelled demo clip and a narrow
   request for usability feedback; target eight participants.
3. Ask moderators before posting in investing, fintech, analytics or learning
   communities; target six participants and never repeat promotional posts.
4. Ask satisfied participants for one consented introduction; target six referrals
   without revealing prior participant identities.

Do not buy ads yet. The early goal is problem and repeat-use validation, not traffic.
Paid acquisition becomes sensible only after the guided journey, clarity, trust and
weekly-interest thresholds are met.

## Pilot scorecard

| Metric | Continue target | Stop or investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full guided-journey completion | at least 80% | below 60% after five sessions |
| Average clarity | at least 4.0 / 5 | below 3.0 / 5 |
| Average trust | at least 4.0 / 5 | below 3.0 / 5 or repeated unsafe interpretation |
| Average perceived value | at least 4.0 / 5 | below 2.5 / 5 |
| Weekly-use interest | at least 70% yes or maybe | at least 50% not relevant |
| Time to first useful insight | five minutes or less | over ten minutes in three sessions |
| Organic follow-up interest | at least 30% request another session | none after ten sessions |

## Four-week rhythm

- Week 1: assign product, privacy, support, measurement, cost and rollback owners;
  test the script internally; schedule sessions only after human approval.
- Week 2: run five to ten sessions and create one privacy-safe aggregate.
- Week 3: fix one major friction and test that change with the next cohort.
- Week 4: aggregate results, document stop/continue evidence and record the owner decision.

## Safety and release boundary

The Phase 9E workspace imports one Phase 9D JSON draft in browser memory and rejects
identity/contact fields, email-like values, inconsistent recommendations, missing
review requirements or changed pilot limits. It can copy reviewed wording and
download an unsigned aggregate launch kit. It cannot send messages, collect contacts,
activate signup, install analytics, provision accounts, collect payment, connect a
live provider, offer personalised financial advice or execute financial actions.

The web-release confirmations are `BUILD_PHASE_9E`, `DEPLOY_PHASE_9E` and
`VERIFY_WEB_PHASE_9E`. Publishing the code does not run or approve any workflow.
