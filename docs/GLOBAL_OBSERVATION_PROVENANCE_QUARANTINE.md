# Global observation provenance and quarantine

Phase 8K defines a fail-closed intake contract between approved external evidence
and the country, dependency and event intelligence foundations. No provider connection or observation release is implied by a source-family record.

## Disconnected source contracts

The foundation defines eight source families: official statistics, central
banks, regulators and law, issuer filings, licensed market data, licensed news,
licensed logistics, and official geoscience and hazard data. Every connector is
`not_connected`; endpoints, credentials, retention, display, derivation and
model-use rights remain unapproved.

Each family has an explicit empty quarantine lane. Candidate, schema-valid,
corroborated, conflicting and released observation counts all begin at zero.
The absence of an approved provider is shown as a gap, not replaced with a
fixture or synthetic value.

## Canonical normalization contracts

Nine contracts preserve source identity, subject identity, field semantics,
value and missingness, unit and currency, geography and scope, observation time,
publication and revision lineage, and license lineage. Ingestion time cannot be
substituted for observation time, unavailable values cannot become zero, and a
similarly named field cannot be treated as equivalent without its definition.

## Quarantine and release gates

Source rights, origin authenticity, subject and field identity, unit and scope,
temporal and revision lineage, schema quality, independent corroboration, and
conflict-aware human release must all pass before a later phase may release an
observation. Disagreement remains explicit; it is not automatically averaged or
resolved.

## Hard boundary

Live connectivity, production ingestion, automatic normalization approval,
automatic conflict resolution, automatic release, model training, autonomous
publication and trade execution are database-constrained off. The reference
contracts are append-only, public access is limited to sanitized readiness
views, and production smoke verification is query-only.

The future activation order is provider-specific rights approval, endpoint and
schema certification, isolated candidate intake, deterministic normalization,
independent corroboration, conflict review, accountable human release, and only
then separately reviewed downstream use. Phase 8K completes only the contract
and empty-quarantine foundation.
