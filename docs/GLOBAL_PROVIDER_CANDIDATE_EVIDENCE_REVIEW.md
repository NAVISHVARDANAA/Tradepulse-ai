# Global provider candidate evidence review

Phase 8N defines the human-review evidence matrix required before a future
provider-specific conformance review may be opened. No provider candidate is
selected, named, connected or approved by this foundation.

## Eight unopened review packets

One provider-empty packet is defined for each of the eight source families.
Every packet remains `not_opened` and `blocked`; it contains no provider name,
legal entity, endpoint, credential, evidence document, payload, reviewer or
approval. A source-family placeholder is not a provider selection.

## Twelve evidence gates

The review checklist covers legal identity and ownership; access and license
rights; display and retention rights; derivation and model-use rights; privacy
and data protection; security and secret isolation; versioned endpoint and
schema mapping; deterministic provider-specific synthetic fixtures; negative
and schema-drift evidence; observed failure drills; availability and change
control; and an accountable signed activation decision.

Each requirement blocks endpoint execution, candidate writes and observation
release. No requirement can pass automatically.

## Evidence-empty matrix

The eight packets and twelve requirements produce ninety-six deterministic
matrix cells. Every cell begins `not_submitted`, with no evidence reference,
reviewer identity, review time, expiry or approval. Public views expose only
these sanitized gaps and never expose evidence documents or secret material.

## Hard boundary

Provider selection, opening a review packet, evidence submission, document
storage, endpoint connectivity, credential storage, external payload intake,
fixture execution, conformance approval, candidate writes, observation release,
model training, autonomous publication and autonomous trading remain
database-constrained off. Reference rows are append-only and production smoke
verification is query-only.

A future provider-specific change must first identify an approved legal owner,
establish rights and privacy/security evidence outside the public application,
provision isolated credentials, submit a versioned field map, run bounded
synthetic and fail-closed tests, record observed failure-drill evidence and
obtain an accountable human activation decision. Phase 8N creates only the
review contract; it creates no ingress or execution path.
