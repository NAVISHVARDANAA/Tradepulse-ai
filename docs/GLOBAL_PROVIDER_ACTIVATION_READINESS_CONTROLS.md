# Global provider activation authorization and change controls

Phase 8S defines the bounded authorization, change-window, abort, restoration
and verification requirements that must exist before a future provider could be
activated. It records no real provider, change packet, authorization, endpoint,
credential, payload, maintenance window or activation.

## Seven reference-only change-control states

The catalog covers `activation_not_requested`, `change_packet_required`,
`independent_authorization_required`, `pre_activation_snapshot_required`,
`bounded_window_required`, `abort_and_verification_required`, and
`closed_without_activation`. These are governance definitions, not a live
change workflow. Automatic transitions are disabled and no state grants
endpoint, credential, write or production effects.

## Eight fail-closed activation gates

Every source family must eventually prove current decision and recovery
readiness, bounded provider identity and scope, legal authority, use rights,
privacy/security/custody, pinned endpoint and schema contracts, rehearsed abort
and restoration, and independent dual-control authorization. All gate templates
are unmet and contain no evidence, reviewer or human authorization.

## Sixty-four blocked readiness cells

The eight provider-neutral source families and eight activation gates create 64
deterministic readiness cells. Every cell is blocked and contains no change
packet, decision, recovery plan, authorizer, reason code, signature, maintenance
window, activation time or verification time. Sanitized public views expose
only these gaps.

## Hard boundary

Activation-request and authorization recording, change-window scheduling,
provider selection, endpoint connectivity, credential storage, payload intake,
fixture execution, conformance approval, provider activation, candidate writes,
observation release, model training, publication and trading remain
database-constrained off. Reference records are append-only and production
smoke verification is query-only.

A future separately authorized provider-specific change must establish verified
identities, current legal and use rights, controlled evidence, isolated secrets,
independent dual control, a bounded expiring window, fail-closed abort,
restoration evidence, immutable audit and accountable post-change verification.
Phase 8S creates activation-readiness scaffolding only; it creates no activation
path.
