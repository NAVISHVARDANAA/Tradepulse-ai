# Controlled-audience pilot operating model

Phase 8X turns the existing launch, live-data and commercial-readiness
foundations into an actionable customer-discovery plan. It does not activate a
cohort. It defines the evidence, operating owners and decision thresholds
required before accountable humans may approve a bounded pilot.

## Two-track strategy

Track A is a free, four-week research and paper-simulation pilot for no more
than 30 pre-approved participants. It uses only already-authorized reference or
delayed data, preserves visible freshness and evidence labels, and cannot place
orders, move money, collect payment or imply personalized advice.

Track B is the real-time-data path. It remains blocked until a provider is
selected, contracted and integrated under the Phase 8V and Phase 8W controls.
Track A must never be described as real time when it is not.

## Target cohorts

1. Eight novice investors: validate clarity, education and trust.
2. Eight self-directed retail investors: validate research workflow value.
3. Six finance or data professionals: validate evidence and methodology.
4. Four fintech or product reviewers: validate positioning and differentiation.
5. Four mobile and accessibility testers: validate inclusive usability.

The repository contains no participant names or personal data. Recruitment,
screening, consent and roster approval remain manual prerequisites.

## Success and safety measures

- At least 70% activation among approved participants.
- Time to first value no greater than ten minutes.
- At least 40% weekly retention across four weeks.
- At least 60% completion of the core research flow.
- Trust-clarity rating of at least 4/5.
- Zero critical safety-boundary violations.
- Support response within one business day.

These are pilot hypotheses, not invented production forecasts. The accountable
owner must approve an actual cost ceiling before recruitment or activation.

## Operating workstreams

The pilot requires eight human-owned workstreams: target-segment definition,
value proposition, pilot offer, recruitment and consent, onboarding and
support, telemetry and learning, unit economics and cost ceiling, and a final
go/no-go review with rollback ownership.

## Fail-closed boundary

Phase 8X enables no invitations, automated provisioning, public signup,
checkout, payment collection, live provider connectivity, production
credentials, production payload intake, live-data display, order routing,
money movement, custody, settlement or automatic go/no-go decision.

The cockpit reads three existing sanitized status views. It creates no new
database migration and preserves migration 066 as the current data contract.
No operational action is exposed through the browser.
