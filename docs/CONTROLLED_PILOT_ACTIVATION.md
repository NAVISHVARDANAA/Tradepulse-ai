# Controlled pilot activation

Phase 8Y converts the controlled-audience operating model into an explicit,
human-owned activation sequence. It does not invite, provision or activate a
participant. It makes the remaining evidence, ownership, wave and stop
decisions visible before a bounded real-user pilot.

## Activation gates

The first invitation requires independent evidence for eight gates:

1. Production origin and authentication.
2. Legal, privacy, risk and consent.
3. Approved participant roster and jurisdiction.
4. Staffed support, monitoring and incident response.
5. Privacy-safe telemetry and interview operations.
6. Data rights, freshness and customer labels.
7. Cost ceiling, accessibility and capacity.
8. Rollback ownership and an accountable go / no-go decision.

The cockpit intentionally reports all gates as verification required. Evidence
and participant identities are not stored in the public browser contract.

## Progressive waves

- Wave 1: five trusted, consented testers.
- Wave 2: ten pre-screened external participants after seven safe days.
- Wave 3: fifteen remaining approved participants after Wave 2 meets safety,
  trust, activation and workflow thresholds.

The total ceiling remains 30 participants over four weeks. A later wave never
inherits approval automatically.

## Data-mode boundary

The controlled pilot can operate with clearly labelled authorized reference or
delayed data. It must not describe that data as real time. Real-time display
remains a separate track requiring a selected and contracted licensed provider,
explicit display and jurisdiction entitlements, isolated credentials, certified
schema and quality controls, independent authorization, and a zero-customer
canary with tested rollback.

## Stop conditions

The pilot pauses or revokes access for a critical security or privacy incident,
an exposed trading or payment path, incorrect real-time labelling, personalized
advice behavior, unresolved data-rights doubt, unstaffed support or monitoring,
a core accessibility or performance block, or reaching the approved cost or
participant ceiling.

## Fail-closed boundary

Phase 8Y enables no invitation delivery, browser enrollment, automatic
provisioning, public signup, live-provider connection, production credential,
production payload, live-data display, order routing, payment, money movement,
custody, settlement or automatic go / no-go transition. The release remains at
database migration 066 and adds no participant PII or operational write path.
