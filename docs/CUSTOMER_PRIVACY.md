# Customer privacy and data rights

Phase 4L gives signed-in customers a private Data Control Center. Optional
product analytics and research updates default to off. Customers can request a
copy of their data or request account deletion without exposing direct identity,
device, network or credential evidence in the privacy control plane.

Rights requests are not executed by the browser. The database records one active
request of each type, requires AAL2 when the account has enrolled MFA, and limits
customers to reading their own status. Completion, rejection and destructive
deletion require a future separately permissioned service and written operating
procedure.

Before external beta launch, define the controller/operator entity, supported
jurisdictions, statutory response windows, identity-verification procedure,
retention exceptions, processor inventory, export format, deletion runbook and
appeal/escalation path. Obtain qualified privacy counsel for each launch region.
