# Global provider review authority and evidence custody

Phase 8P defines the accountable roles, separation of duties and sealed-evidence
lifecycle required before any real provider-candidate review can begin. No
reviewer, evidence custodian, provider candidate or evidence location is
assigned by this foundation.

## Eight unassigned review roles

The governance catalog defines a candidate accountable owner, legal entity
reviewer, licensing and rights reviewer, privacy and data-protection reviewer,
security and evidence custodian, schema and data-contract reviewer, conformance
and operations reviewer, and a separate activation authority.

Every role remains `unassigned`, stores no identity and blocks packet opening,
endpoint access and release. The activation authority cannot replace the
independent domain reviews, and no role can authorize itself through the public
application.

## Seven-stage sealed-evidence lifecycle

The reference lifecycle covers `not_received`, `received_sealed`, `triaged`,
`under_review`, `needs_remediation`, `accepted_until_expiry`, and
`expired_or_revoked`. These rows specify a future controlled process only.
They do not represent receipt, storage, review or acceptance of real evidence.

Every transition requires accountable human action. Automatic transitions are
disabled, no custody location is provisioned and every stage has zero
production effect.

## Responsibility matrix

The eight provider-neutral source families and eight role templates produce
sixty-four deterministic responsibility cells. Every cell is unassigned and
unauthorized, with no reviewer identity, custody location, assignment time,
authorization time or expiry. Sanitized public views expose only those gaps.

## Hard boundary

Role assignment, reviewer identity storage, evidence receipt, evidence document
storage, custody provisioning, provider selection, review-packet opening,
evidence submission, endpoint connectivity, credential storage, external
payload intake, fixture execution, conformance approval, candidate writes,
observation release, model training, autonomous publication and autonomous
trading remain database-constrained off. Reference rows are append-only and
production smoke verification is query-only.

A future authorized change must establish approved people and organizations,
least-privilege custody, audit retention, independent domain review, explicit
expiry and revocation, and dual-control activation before it may receive real
evidence or open a provider-specific packet. Phase 8P creates governance
scaffolding only; it creates no evidence ingress, provider connection or
execution path.
